Frequently asked questions

Can I order products through the website?

You can build a list of products and send it to a branch on WhatsApp so it can confirm availability and price. Payment and pickup are arranged with the branch. (sample text)

Do medicines need a prescription?

Some medicines are dispensed only with a valid prescription under Saudi regulations. Ask the pharmacist at your branch. (sample text, to be reviewed with the organisation)

Does the site store my health data?

No. The site does not collect or store health or personal data on any server. Please do not type sensitive medical information into contact forms.

How do I find a branch's opening hours?

The Branches page shows each branch's hours and whether it is open now (Riyadh time). Current hours are samples.
